## 5. Rule compliance recap

- **Rule 1 (data isolation):** factor fundamentals come through the existing
  CVForge FMP passthrough — no new vendor (ADR-005).
- **Rule 4 (FlashAlpha):** LETF issuance, factor scores, credit ranking are all
  descriptors; the only signal writer is `strategies/swing_options.py`, gated on a
  matured IV-rank percentile (never a raw GEX/DEX crossing) and flagged
  experimental.
- **Rule 5 (idempotency):** `fundamentals_snapshots` upsert on (symbol, ts); the
  swing-signals job guards once-per-day.
- **Rule 7 (local/cost):** factor compute is pure numpy — no LLM in the scored path.
