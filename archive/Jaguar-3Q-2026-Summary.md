## 2. 2H 2026 Macro Outlook

The note is written as Fahad grading his own December 2025 call for a **"broadening of the bull market"** — Mag-7 underperforming the S&P-493, small caps, and reasonably-priced growth. Verdict: the rotation thesis is working; the macro backdrop it counted on is not.

**The three pillars, graded:**

- **Pillar 1 — Broaden AI exposure: VALIDATED.** Equal-weight RSP +9.9% vs. SPY +7.6%; MAGS −5–6% YTD (worst month on record in June). The engine is the earnings crossover: ex-NVIDIA, the "Mag-6" are projected to grow CY2026 EPS just **13.2% — below the S&P 493's 15.9%.** The growth premium that justified 30x+ mega-cap multiples is converging away. (Caveat: not yet Street consensus — Morgan Stanley still models Mag-7 ahead until Q4 2026.)
- **Pillar 2 — Small-cap recovery: VALIDATED (strongest pillar).** Russell 2000 +21% YTD, crossing 3,000 for the first time. Refinement: with the rate tailwind gone, **tilt to quality — favor S&P 600 (IJR) over broad Russell 2000 (IWM)** because "high rates kill zombies" (~40% of the R2K is unprofitable / floating-rate).
- **Pillar 3 — GARP rotation: PARTIAL.** Two-speed. **Regional banks (KRE)** lead on a re-steepened 2s/10s curve (~+27–31 bps), ~11x forward. **Healthcare (XLV)** staged a June breakout (share of constituents above 50-DMA jumped 25%→61%). **Homebuilders (ITB/XHB)** remain stuck — buydown costs, rising inventory, ~27% material tariffs; a coiled spring waiting on the 10-year to break below ~4.30%.

**What changed — the macro regime flip (rate-cut dream → rate-hike risk):**

1. **Iran war / oil shock** — conflict from late Feb 2026 closed the Strait of Hormuz (~20% of seaborne oil), Brent peaked >$113; a fragile mid-June ceasefire since collapsed WTI back to high-$60s/low-$70s, but the 1H inflation damage is done.
2. **Inflation re-accelerated** — May CPI +4.2% YoY (hottest since Apr 2023, third straight monthly acceleration; energy +23.5%). Core held 2.9% — largely a supply shock, but 4.2% headline poisoned the well for cuts.
3. **New hawkish Fed Chair** — Kevin Warsh sworn in late May; June 17 FOMC read as regime change (held 3.50–3.75%, killed forward guidance, "isn't particularly restrictive," 9–9 split, median dot now points to a **hike**). BofA reversed to call **three 25bp hikes** in 2H26; CME FedWatch put a December hike near 88%.

**Out-of-consensus angle — the Phase-2 capex air pocket:** Hyperscaler AI capex is guided to ~$700–725B in 2026 (+77% YoY), but the market is starting to punish spend without visible return (Meta −9% on a capex raise). Every dollar of hyperscaler capex becomes another company's opex savings — so the capex digestion that threatens Mag-7 multiples is exactly what *funds* Pillar 1: value rotates from Phase 1 (AI infrastructure — NVIDIA/hyperscalers) to **Phase 2 (AI application — the under-owned 493 and GARP names capturing productivity).**

**Five moves for 2H:** (1) stay overweight broadening via RSP; (2) hold small-cap overweight but tilt to quality (IJR > IWM); (3) within GARP lead with KRE + the XLV breakout, bench homebuilders; (4) hedge the macro tail — if BofA's 75bp hike scenario builds, rotate to energy, financials, the dollar and cut duration-sensitive growth; (5) re-underwrite AI-infrastructure names for Phase-2 digestion and rotate toward the adopters.

## 3. The 13 Premium Ideas

All 13 are **long / bullish** — despite the "Bullish, Bearish" section header, no short idea appears. Jaguar does not publish its own price targets for Top Picks (it simulates a quarterly-refreshed common-stock portfolio); the targets below are third-party sell-side figures cited within each write-up. Several ideas are explicit "beaten-down / mispriced recovery" longs (RACE, ISRG, MELI, SOLV, TEM, NESR).

### AXON — Axon Enterprise *(large-cap compounder)*
Not just a TASER company — a cloud-connected public-safety OS selling into ~17,000 of ~18,000 U.S. police forces. **$14.3B contracted backlog (>5 years of revenue)**; cloud ~44% of revenue at >80% gross margin. Growth vectors: AI Era/Draft One, DFR/Dedrone drones, Prepared dispatch, enterprise (ABW Mini), international (EMEA). GAAP EPS about to inflect. *Catalysts:* AI Era bookings > 2025's $750M; Body 5 (25x compute) by 2027; $6B 2028 revenue target. *PTs cited:* MS $600 / Piper $674 / TD Cowen $825. *Flow:* thin — 220 Jul-500 calls bought May 26.

### CHRW — C.H. Robinson *(freight-cycle + AI margin leverage)*
Largest N.A. freight broker, asset-light. A 43-month freight recession is inflecting on **supply-side capacity contraction** (small-carrier bankruptcies, driver shortage), driving tender rejections and spot-market spread capture. Underappreciated angle: an agentic-AI routing platform lets one engineer handle ~4x volume, decoupling cost from growth → margin beats. *Catalysts:* Tender Rejection Index >15% (from ~5% trough); ACT Driver Index 30.4; AI penetration >85% of N.A. surface transport; cost-to-serve −17% YoY. *PT:* none.

### CMPS — Compass Pathways *(regulatory-inflection biotech)*
COMP360 (synthetic psilocybin) for treatment-resistant depression (~4M U.S. adults; Spravato only ~2.5% penetrated). Three successful controlled trials; Stifel puts approval odds at 90%. An April 2026 Executive Order + Commissioner's National Priority Voucher could compress FDA review to 1–2 months → possible **approval in early 2027**, ahead of Street's 2028. ~90% of U.S. population in states expected to reschedule within 30 days of DEA action. *Catalysts:* COMP006 Part B 26-week data early Q3; NDA submission Q4; approval early 2027. *PT:* none (HCW 2027 revenue est. $147.8M vs. $56.2M consensus — 2.6x gap).

### RACE — Ferrari *(quality-at-a-discount recovery)*
Down ~30% from €447.50 to ~€312 on three overstated fears — the polarizing Luce EV design, a "conservative" 5% CAGR reframe, and soft hybrid residuals — none of which break the model. Real driver: price/mix + personalization (~20% of car revenue) and the F80/849 Testarossa/296 Speciale ramp. A clean K-shaped-economy play with multi-year order-book visibility. *Catalysts:* three more 2026 model reveals; Luce deliveries Q4; F80 ramp; MS Q2 price/mix +7.5% YoY. *PTs cited:* BofA €350 / GS €383 / MS €380 base, €450 bull.

### GTX — Garrett Motion *(re-rating from turbo-decline to industrial growth)*
World's #1 turbo supplier, mispriced as a "melting ice cube." Garrett cut its 2030 BEV assumption 41%→27% and expects more turbo units in 2035 than 2025. The real story is a new industrial-compressor engine (oil-free centrifugal) with three blue-chip partners (Trane, Tonfy, Ingersoll Rand) plus a near-invisible AI **data-center liquid-cooling** opportunity (~$7B e-cooling TAM floor). *Catalysts:* partner rollouts 2026–27; a named data-center cooling win (biggest re-rate trigger); industrial >$500M by 2030 / >$850M by 2035. *PT:* none. *Flow:* >$1.5M of calls bought in May (Oct-29s, Oct-33s, Jan-27 39s).

### ISRG — Intuitive Surgical *(mispriced ~30% drawdown)*
Soft-tissue robotic-surgery leader (da Vinci dV5, SP, Ion) down ~30% YTD on three overstated fears — slowing procedures, instrument-pricing headwinds, remanufactured competition. Force Feedback life-extension (6→15 uses) is a **gross-margin tailwind**, not a revenue hit; the instrument-price playbook mirrors 2020 (small per-procedure haircut → explosive volume). Expert sees ISRG holding ≥80% share. *Catalysts:* 2026 procedure guide raised to 13.5–15.5% (highest in large-cap MedTech); ASC expansion; **$5B buyback** (first since 2017). *PT:* none.

### LPTH — LightPath Technologies *(defense / material-substitution ramp)*
Optical-components + IR/thermal-imaging maker, now >50% defense. Its **BlackDiamond** chalcogenide glass is a domestic, NDAA-compliant substitute for Germanium (China controls ~80% of supply; ~$8,597/kg vs. BlackDiamond ~$2,000/kg — a >4x structural gap). Multiple defense programs accelerating at once; backlog grew ~$98M→$103M in under a month; an unmodeled space-telescope TAM. *Catalysts:* airborne BlackDiamond system contract award expected late summer/early autumn 2026; new federal budget → missile orders; ~$30M Counter-UAS backlog. *PT:* none. *Flow:* fresh call buying (Dec-20s, Jan-27 30s, Sept-15s).

### LQDT — Liquidity Services *(counter-cyclical margin inflection)*
World's largest B2B surplus-asset marketplace — benefits from tariff-driven inventory front-running and the eventual glut. Fiscal Q2 EPS $0.39 beat $0.28 (~40%), EBITDA +37% YoY. **Self-Service model (near-100% gross margin) crossed the >80% GMV threshold (81%)** — the inflection where EBITDA growth outpaces revenue. GovDeals/Bid4Assets public-sector engine set for Q3 seasonal acceleration. *Catalysts:* Section 301 tariff outcomes; restructuring-driven CAG/RSCG volumes; GovDeals seasonality. *PT:* none (~13x EBITDA, expected to re-rate).

### MELI — MercadoLibre *(transitory-margin recovery)*
LatAm e-commerce + fintech leader sold off near 52-week lows on net margin 9.2%→6.9%, driven mainly by Mercado Pago's credit-book hyper-growth where IFRS 9 forces Day-1 full expected-loss recognition (NIMAL compressed to 17.8% in Q1). These pressures are transitory: as 2H25/1H26 card cohorts season past 6 months, provisioning drops and margins should expand from Q3. *Catalysts:* provisions ease in Q3 (90+ day NPLs improved to 8.0%, coverage 149%); pending **Mexico banking license** (2H26 likely); XEM3 logistics automation scaling 550K→~1M packages/day. *PT:* none.

### NESR — National Energy Services Reunited *(mispriced MENA OFS)*
Pure-play MENA oilfield-services (Aramco, ADNOC, KOC). Intentional geographic concentration is a moat as NOCs reward continuity. Headline driver: Aramco's **Jafurah** unconventional-gas contract (5-yr, one of the world's largest), already ramping — Q1 2026 revenue a record $404.6M (+33.5% YoY). Management targets $2B run-rate soon, $3B in 2–3 years, on signed multi-year contracts — yet trades sub-5x 2027 EBITDA. *Catalysts:* Jafurah + new awards; $300M cementing wins; ADNOC $55B pledge; **first-ever capital-return framework** (+$50M buyback). *PT:* none.

### SOLV — Solventum *(activist sum-of-the-parts)*
Healthcare conglomerate spun from 3M (Apr 2024, $8.33B FY25 revenue) at a deep discount (~11x fwd P/E, ~9x EBITDA) despite 7,000+ patents, >90% retention, 37%+ ROE. **Nelson Peltz's Trian (~5%)** is moving fast — already forced the $4.1B P&F sale to Thermo Fisher and accelerated 3M TSA exits; an April 30 letter now demands separation of HIS and Dental, leaving a focused ~$5B MedSurg pure-play worth peer multiples. *Catalysts:* Trian campaign → portfolio action; TSA exit ~90% by FY26; ~20 launches over 24 months. *PT cited:* Wedbush $94 (bull $119).

### SLB — SLB (Schlumberger) *(deepwater renaissance + AI optionality)*
World's largest oilfield-services co. (81% of sales ex-N.A.), leveraged to a deepwater/offshore renaissance (Brazil, Guyana, West Africa, GoM, North Sea) via quiet contract wins (PETRONAS Suriname ~$2.2B subsea, Beacon Shenandoah, CNOOC Kaiping, PTTEP Kikeh, Aker JV). ChampionX synergies + an underappreciated Digital & Integration/AI business (DELFI, NVIDIA "AI Factory for Energy," data-center cooling). *Catalysts:* 2026 FID pipeline heading >$100B, step-up in 2027; ChampionX $400M synergy target; **D&I ARR passed $1.0B** (+15% YoY), data-center solutions +45% YoY. *PT:* none.

### TEM — Tempus AI *(financial inflection in 2H26)*
Precision-medicine/health-tech with one of the world's largest clinical+molecular oncology datasets (45M+ records, 500+ PB). Down >50% from ~$104 (2025 peak) to ~5x forward sales on fears of widening losses and LLM disruption — both misconstrued. The "slowdown" to 25% 2026 growth is optical (lapping the Feb 2025 Ambry acquisition); demand is robust (RCV >$1B). **First full year of positive EBITDA (~$65M FY26 guide)** on operating leverage + high-margin Data & Applications mix + an ASP shift from LDT to FDA-approved xT-CDx. MRD portfolio is an added underappreciated driver. *Catalysts:* first positive EBITDA; back-half-weighted Data & Apps monetization; MRD ramp (liquid-biopsy MRD volumes +500% YoY). *PT:* none (~5x fwd sales; 2026 guide ~$1.60B).
